Panels (a) and (b) show the reversal trace for the zero-reset (time constant 0.125 s) and tracking-reset (time constant 0.25 s) variants: 256 steps of dt = 1/32 s, input -8 rad/s, then +8 rad/s from t = 4 s. The dashed line is the exact rational intent and the solid line with circles is the binary32 compiled C output; the two agree at every step, so the solid line lies on the dashed one. Crosses mark the 2 steps per variant (t = 2 s and t = 5 s) where the state reaches the rate clamp of 2 rad/s in magnitude. Their uncertainty interval touches the branch threshold, so the branch requirement stays open; all implementations took the same branch at these steps. Panels (c) and (d) show the constant-input trace, 64 steps of dt = 1/32 s at 0.5 rad/s, on a log scale: the solid line with circles is the absolute error of compiled C against exact intent and the dash-dot line is the deterministic worst-case bound from NUMERICS.md. The error stays at least 324 times (zero-reset) and 897 times (tracking-reset) below the bound. Both variants use slew limits +4 and -2 rad/s² and the same rate clamp.
